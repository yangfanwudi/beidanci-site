Words you haven't truly learned never consume your daily quota, and finished groups flow into the next until the day is done. Every word comes with an AI explainer card, AI quizzes and an always-on Ask AI. 特核心特性Features

为「记住」设计，而不是为「背完」 Built for remembering, not just for finishing

全部特性围绕一件事：让每一次复习都出现在快要忘记的时候。 Every feature serves one goal: each review lands right before you'd forget.

讲

AI 讲解卡AI explainer cards

每个单词都有一张 AI 讲解卡：词根拆解 + 谐音记忆 + 两条情景例句。比如 obligation = ob（向）+ lig（捆绑）+ ation →「把人捆绑住的东西」→ 责任。 Every word comes with an AI explainer card: morphological breakdown, a homophone mnemonic and two contextual sentences. Take obligation = ob (toward) + lig (bind) → “the thing that binds you” → a duty.

测

AI 测验AI quizzes

秒级生成混合题型的测验（实测 8 题约 5 秒出完）。干扰项不是随机凑数，而是 assemble、dissolve 这类真实易混词，答案分布均匀。 Mixed-format quizzes generated in seconds — in testing, 8 questions in about 5. Distractors aren't random filler but genuinely confusable words like assemble or dissolve, with evenly spread answers.

问

问 AIAsk AI

学习中随时追问，秒级回答。比如问 obligation 和 responsibility 有什么区别，回答分点、有条理，当场消化疑惑。 Ask follow-ups any time during a session and get an answer in seconds. Ask how obligation differs from responsibility, for example, and get a structured, point-by-point reply.

故

AI 小故事AI mini stories

每天把当日学过的词编成一篇小故事，读完等于再复习一遍（生成走 Gemini，与测验同一条已验证链路；当日缓存优先，打开即读）。 Each day's words are woven into a short story — reading it is one more review. Generated by Gemini on the same verified pipeline as the quiz, served from cache once made.

复

间隔重复调度Spaced-repetition scheduling

每张卡片按遗忘曲线安排下次复习时间。打开学习页时，到期的复习词先出现，然后才是今天的新词——顶部会明确告诉你队列是「复习 X · 新词 Y」。 Every card schedules its next review along the forgetting curve. Due reviews always come before new words, and the study page tells you exactly what's in the queue: “Review X · New Y”.

额

每日新词名额A daily quota of new words

每天只有固定数量的新词名额。新词点「重来」不算学会：不建卡、不占名额，当天稍后回到队列里，直到真正学会为止。 Each day brings a fixed quota of new words. Rating a new word “Again” doesn't create a card or consume quota — it stays a new word and returns later the same day until you actually learn it.

评

三档评分 · 诚实计数Three grades, honest counting

每张卡片自评「重来 / 良好 / 简单」。「重来」的卡片回到本组末尾再出现一次，但不计入「已完成」——进度条永远不会虚高。 Rate each card Again, Good, or Easy. An “Again” card comes back at the end of its group but never counts as completed — the progress bar never lies.

续

学完一组，自动续下一组Auto-continue to the next group

学完一组而当天还有名额时，自动拉取下一组继续学；只有当天的名额全部用完，才会看到「今日任务完成」。进度统计跨组累计。 Finished a group but still have quota left? The next group loads automatically. The completion page only appears when the whole day's quota is truly done, with progress counted across groups.

典

五本词书 · 完整词义Five word books, full definitions

内置五本词书。单词详情包含中文释义、带词性标签的英文释义、例句与近/反义词；不在词书里的词（如变形词）也会自动翻译出中文词义，并标明是词典补充内容。 Five word books built in. Word pages show Chinese definitions, English definitions with part-of-speech tags, example sentences, and synonyms/antonyms. Words outside the books (like inflected forms) get an auto-translated Chinese gloss, clearly marked as a dictionary supplement.

装

可安装的 PWAAn installable PWA

以 Web 应用形式开发，可安装到桌面或主屏。页面与静态资源离线缓存，学习队列与统计等接口永远直连服务器，不会拿到过期数据。 Built as a web app you can install to your desktop or home screen. Pages and static assets are cached for offline use, while study queues and stats always hit the server directly — never stale data.

工程实践Engineering practices

测试覆盖Test coverage

SRS 建卡规则是纯函数并配有单元测试；词典的词性解析同样有测试覆盖。 The SRS card-creation rule is a pure function with unit tests; the dictionary's part-of-speech parsing is tested too.

缓存纪律Cache discipline

Service Worker 只缓存页面与静态资源，任何 /api/* 接口一律直连服务器。 The service worker caches pages and static assets only — every /api/* request bypasses the cache.

文档驱动Document-driven

每次修改都记录版本、文件与原因；错误与预防措施归档成册，避免重蹈覆辙。 Every change is logged with its version, files, and reasons; errors and preventions are archived so the same mistakes don't repeat.
